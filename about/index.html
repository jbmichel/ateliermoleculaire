AtelierMoléculaire

AI-driven molecular innovation for beauty brands.

01What

AI is making exponential progress in technical fields including industrial engineering, materials discovery, and biopharma. Early-stage drug discovery is being transformed by models powerful enough to reason about molecular structures.

Beauty brands are at the forefront of the mounting consumer demand for newer, more personalized molecules that better connect wellness to health. We are building the platform to meet this demand, and bring the cutting edge of molecule discovery to beauty brands: new and better molecules and formulations, engineered faster and with greater precision.

02How

We partner with beauty brands to build new molecules and formulations that meet their objectives, with increasingly higher performance.

We build a lab-in-the-loop for beauty, so that we can best discover new peptides and molecules with optimal lab data, both in terms of safety and efficacy. By combining AI design with in vitro experiments, we explore vastly more design space than possible otherwise, guided by concrete lab data.

03Who

Founding team with deep expertise in AI, engineering, molecular design, consumer goods, and beauty. Serial entrepreneurs with several exits and long scientific, operational, and business track records.

04When

Now. We expect to work with a small number of design partners as we build our platform.
